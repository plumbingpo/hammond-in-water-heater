Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Schererville

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve once a year to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling once a year if their water is hard.

The age of your heater matters just as much. With regular service, a standard tank typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like discolored hot water, rumbling, or moisture under the tank, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and causes far less stress than an emergency swap after a leak.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to excess pressure or thermal expansion with nowhere to go, both inexpensive fixes. The same drip ignored for months can keep stressing the tank until a seam gives way, and a small repair becomes a full replacement plus water damage. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and ruined floors.

What Goes Wrong With Local Water Heaters

Whatever type of water heater your Schererville home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks lose elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. Our technicians work on all four every week and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
