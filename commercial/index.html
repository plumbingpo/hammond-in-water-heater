Restaurant Services

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and a high-temperature dish machine depends on a booster to reach its sanitizing temperature. Hammond Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we schedule service around your hours.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. We verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, so hot water is ready when the first shift arrives.

Heavy-Use Systems

Hotel, Multifamily, and Large-Facility Water Heaters

Scale changes everything. One failed tank in a hotel can leave an entire floor with lukewarm showers, and an apartment building with a single central heater needs a real plan, not random service calls. Hammond Water Heater brings the know-how and structure these systems require, from recirculation pumps and mixing valves to staged installations.

For a large facility, downtime is measured in lost revenue and tenant complaints, so we plan for prevention first. That means comprehensive system assessments, sizing based on real occupancy, multiple heaters piped in parallel so one failed unit never leaves the building cold, recirculation pump and valve service, and scheduled flushing and descaling to hold recovery. If a unit does go down, our water heater repair technicians isolate it so the rest of the system keeps running.

Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in tanks stored too cool, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Preventive Care

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Hammond Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve tests, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties on a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and more consistent water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
