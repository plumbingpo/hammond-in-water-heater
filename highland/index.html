Keeping Your Highland Water Heater Running Longer

A little routine care makes all the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: an annual sediment flush, an anode inspection every few years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a temperature setting around 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms like rusty hot water, popping and rumbling, moisture around the base, or hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one with water on the floor.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Take a spent anode rod: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water begins corroding the tank itself, and just like that a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a flooded utility room.

Hot Water Headaches Highland Homeowners Face

Whatever type of water heater your Highland home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor trouble, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. Our technicians work on all four every week and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
