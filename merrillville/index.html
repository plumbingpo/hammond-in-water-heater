Keeping Your Merrillville Water Heater Running Longer

Routine maintenance is the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode inspection every few years, a quick relief valve test once a year, a thermostat set near 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so small issues get caught early.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms such as rusty hot water, popping and rumbling, dampness under the tank, or hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one with water on the floor.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Consider a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but once it's gone, the water starts eating the tank steel, and just like that a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a surprise cleanup.

Common Water Heater Problems in Merrillville

Whatever type of water heater your Merrillville home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, dirty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric units burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale buildup, ignition failures, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
